The story

A facility that was sealed
for a reason.

Ward 9 of the Blackmoor Sublevel Facility was decommissioned in 1987 after an incident nobody signed off on. The corridors are still lit. The generators still hum. And patient records still rotate on the wall, as if someone kept working after the staff were gone.

You are the last intake. One key card lock stands between you and the surface lift: the maintenance key from the laboratory, and the blast door in the generator room. The creature that lives in the vents knows these corridors better than you do — it walks them in complete darkness and it only needs to touch you once.

  • 01
    Take the key

    Find the maintenance key in the laboratory on the east wing.

  • 02
    Reach the blast door

    The exit stays locked — and lit red — until the key is yours.

  • 03
    Don't be caught

    Sprinting is loud. The flashlight is a beacon. Choose when to be a target.

Survival rules

  • 01 Sprint drains stamina — walk when it runs dry.
  • 02 The flashlight reveals the facility and reveals you.
  • 03 The exit door is red and sealed until the key is in your hand.

The facility

Four rooms, one way out

Real-time 3D, in the browser

Every corridor, crate and pipe is genuine Three.js geometry with dynamic lighting, fog and flickering fixtures — no background image tricks.

Ward 9 — where you wake

Gurneys, lockers and cold storage. Loud floors, no cover. Keep moving and keep quiet.

The laboratory

Fluorescent haze and a single maintenance key on a pedestal. Everyone who took it was heard doing so.

The theatre

An operating theatre turned supply dump. Crates give you cover — and they give the creature a reason to circle.

The generator room

The blast door sits under a red light that turns green only for you. Hope the generator holds its flicker.

Something is hunting

A procedural creature that patrols, listens, investigates and sprints. It navigates the corridors with a real navigation graph.

The lift is two corridors away.

Best played with sound on and the lights off. Your progress is not saved — every run starts on the same cold gurney.
